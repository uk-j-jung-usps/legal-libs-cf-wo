Standards Applied
=================

- All queries use queryExecute() with parameterized bindings (no inline <cfqueryparam>)
- Modern cfscript operators (!= instead of NEQ, && instead of AND, > instead of GT)
- XSS protection via encodeForHTMLAttribute() / encodeForHTML()
- Null-safe email lookups with recordCount > 0 checks
- try/catch for SSN queries that may fail on restricted tables
- Single <cfscript> blocks — no mixed tag/script
